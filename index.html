Questions, answered.

Does it use a lot of battery?

In typical use (Auto mode, background recording), TripRewind draws about the same as Google Maps or Strava — around 5% per hour on an Android phone. That's roughly 20 hours of continuous recording on a full charge, enough for most full-day trips. Flying uses far less. If you charge your phone once a day, you're covered.

Some locations are sensitive — can I set privacy zones?

Yes. Add a privacy zone around any location — 250m by default, any distance you like — and TripRewind will never share paths inside that zone in an export or share card. The points still live in your secure backup, just not in anything you share. Privacy zones are a free feature, always.

Are trips flexible?

Very. A trip can span months or minutes, and trips can overlap freely. On a six-week European odyssey, you might have one trip covering the lot, another for a single day, and several inside that same day. They all fit together, with your notes and photos attached. Relive any of them, forever — and share any of them.

Does it work without mobile data?

TripRewind records using your phone's GPS chip, which works independently of mobile data. You need a data connection to view map tiles, but recording itself requires no internet.

What happens if my phone restarts mid-trip?

Recording resumes automatically when the phone boots. Any gap will be small and clearly marked in your data.

Can I use it on a hiking trail with no signal?

Yes. GPS works anywhere with sky visibility. No signal required.

What if I lose my phone or switch devices?

Restore your complete history to a new device from your encrypted backup. If it's a different phone, you'll need your recovery phrase — generated when you first back up. Keep it in your password manager and you can move your data to another phone whenever you like.

Is there a web version?

No. Location is a phone thing more than a PC thing, so we built for Android and iPhone. Your data stays on your device — which means no web dashboard, by design.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Clear this device. This securely wipes all recorded points — you're always in control of what's on your phone. If you just want your data off temporarily (say, while crossing a border): back up, clear the device, and delete TripRewind. Once you're through, reinstall and restore the backup. Restoring to a different device always requires your recovery phrase.
